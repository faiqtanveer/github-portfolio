01 About

Turning designs into apps people actually use.

I'm a Flutter developer with 5+ years of experience building cross-platform mobile applications across government, streaming, fintech, mobility, healthcare, and education.

Right now I'm a Senior Flutter Developer at AQZ Solutions, building streaming apps across phones, Apple TV, Fire TV, Android TV and Roku, and at Macquires, where I lead Flutter development on a government HR platform for Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Economy and Planning.
